This Unity pack contains 4 custom shaders:

2 Toon shaders

2 Normal (non-toon) shaders

Each shader type has the same functionality, just with different visual styles (toon vs normal). Here's a breakdown of what each shader does:

1. Toon Face Shader
Used for character faces. This shader allows you to customize facial features with a layered approach:

Eyes and Mouth are split into two textures:

Texture 1: White background with black details – used to color the black parts.

Texture 2: Fully black – used to color the white parts.

Customization options:

General color

Shade color

This setup gives you fine control over eye design and facial color styling in a toon style.

2. Toon Basic Shader
Used for the body, clothes, and props of the character.

Customization options:

General color

Shade color

Base color texture

Ideal for creating consistent stylized visuals for the character’s body and gear.

The two Normal shaders perform the same functions as above but without the toon lighting and stylization effects. Use them if you prefer a more traditional look.

Enjoy customizing your characters!
